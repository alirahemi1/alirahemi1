### ⭐ Featured project: [the digital side of a local detailing business](https://github.com/alirahemi1/velux-detailing-case-study)

My brother runs [Velux Detailing](https://www.veluxdetailing.co.uk), a mobile car detailing business in the West Midlands. I built and run the digital side:

- **Instant online quotes** on 12 pages, with confirmation texts
- **Stripe payments** for gift vouchers, prepaid packs and a monthly subscription, each creating a code and texting the customer automatically
- **A refer-a-friend scheme** with rules that can't be gamed
- **A phone-first jobs app** for him, with two-factor sign-in enforced by the database
- **Analytics** across Google Analytics, Search Console and bookings, which showed 55% of visits came from ads and guided where to spend next
